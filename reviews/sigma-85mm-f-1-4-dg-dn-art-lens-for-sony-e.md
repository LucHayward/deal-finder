# Sigma 85mm f/1.4 DG DN Art Lens for Sony E

**Listing price: R15,000**

DxOMark: N/A — DxOMark has not published a lab score for the DG DN (mirrorless-native) version.
For context, the older DG HSM Art (DSLR) scored 51 — the highest in their 85mm database at the time.

## Pros
- Tack-sharp across the frame; corners beat the Sony FE 85mm f/1.4 GM
- Fast, quiet stepping-motor AF — on par with native Sony lenses
- Excellent bokeh (11-blade aperture) and strong CA control
- Compact and light for an f/1.4 — significantly smaller than the DSLR-era Art
- Aperture ring with de-click switch; weather-sealed mount; premium build

## Cons
- Noticeable pincushion distortion and heavy vignetting wide open (corrected in-camera/RAW)
- No distance scale or hard focus stops
- Large for a mirrorless prime; heavier than Sony's f/1.8 option

## Best Use Cases
Portrait, wedding, event, and low-light photography. Ideal for Sony A7/A9 series shooters wanting GM-level performance at roughly half the price.
