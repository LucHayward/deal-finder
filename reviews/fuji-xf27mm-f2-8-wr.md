# Fujifilm XF 27mm F2.8 R WR

DxOMark: N/A — neither the WR nor the original version has a scored lab review on DxOMark (only specs/measurements are listed). Best-in-class Fujifilm X-mount lens on DxOMark is the XF 50-140mm F2.8 at ~47 points for context.

**Listing price: R6,000**

## Pros
- Genuinely pancake-sized (23mm long, 78g) — transforms any X-body into a pocketable kit
- Weather-sealed with aperture ring — rare combo at this size and price
- Sharp center wide open; good bokeh for f/2.8 on APS-C when shooting close
- 41mm equivalent is a natural, versatile field of view
- Fast, accurate AF for stills (near-silent)

## Cons
- Soft corners wide open; sharpens up around f/4.5
- Occasional chromatic aberration / colored fringing in high-contrast areas
- f/2.8 max aperture limits low-light and subject separation vs. XF 23mm/35mm f/1.4
- AF struggles with fast-moving subjects
- No focal scale or manual focus switch on the lens body

## Best Use Cases
- Street and travel photography where size matters most
- Everyday walk-around / documentary shooting
- Casual portraiture and lifestyle at close-to-medium distances
- Pairing with compact bodies (X-E4, X-T30) for a minimal kit
