# Fujifilm XF 35mm F2 R WR (Black)

**DxOMark:** N/A — this lens has not been tested by DxOMark. For context, the best-scoring Fujifilm X-mount prime on DxOMark is the XF 56mm F1.2 R at 44 points.

**Listing price:** R3,900

## Pros
- Exceptionally compact and lightweight (170g, 45mm long) with full metal build
- Weather-resistant (WR) — rated to -10°C, dust and moisture sealed
- Fast, near-silent stepping motor AF; outperforms the older XF 35mm F1.4 in speed
- Sharp centre performance wide open at F2; holds up on 40MP sensors (X-T5)
- 9-blade rounded aperture produces smooth, pleasing bokeh

## Cons
- F2 max aperture — one stop slower than the XF 35mm F1.4 sibling
- Some chromatic aberration / fringing at edges, especially in colour work
- 43mm filter thread is an uncommon size — harder to find filters
- Corner sharpness trails the centre noticeably at wide apertures

## Best Use Cases
Street photography, travel, documentary, everyday carry, portraits on APS-C (≈53mm FF equivalent). Pairs well with any WR Fujifilm body for outdoor/inclement weather shooting.
