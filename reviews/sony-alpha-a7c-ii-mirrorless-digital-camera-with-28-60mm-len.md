# Sony Alpha a7C II Mirrorless Digital Camera with 28-60mm Lens

**Listing price: R27,999**

DxOMark: N/A — DxOMark has not published a lab score for the Sony FE 28-60mm F4-5.6 lens. For context, top-scoring standard zooms on DxOMark (e.g. Sigma 24-70mm F2.8 DG DN Art) reach scores of ~50+.

## Pros
- Best-in-class AI subject tracking AF (people, animals, vehicles) — locks and holds reliably
- 33MP full-frame BSI-CMOS sensor with excellent image quality and 10-bit 4:2:2 video
- 7EV IBIS — significantly better than the original a7C (5EV)
- Compact rangefinder-style body; smallest/lightest FF mirrorless in its class
- 28-60mm kit lens is weather-sealed, ultra-light (167g), and optically solid for its size

## Cons
- Significant rolling shutter in e-shutter mode and 4K video when panning
- 4K/60p requires 1.5x crop (Super35 mode)
- Single SD card slot only
- EVF resolution (2.36M dot) is low for the price — no higher-res panel exists at this size
- No AF joystick; battery life down ~25% vs original a7C

## Best Use Cases
- Travel and street photography where size/weight matters
- Hybrid shooters wanting compact FF with serious video specs
- Wildlife/pet/sports photographers who need reliable subject tracking
- Vloggers and content creators (Auto Framing, USB-C streaming, S-Cinetone)
