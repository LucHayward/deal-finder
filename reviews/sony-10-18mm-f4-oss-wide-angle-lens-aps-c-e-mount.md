# Sony E 10-18mm F4 OSS (APS-C E-mount)

**Listing price: R7,500**

DxOMark: 15 (best-in-class APS-C wide angle zoom on Sony E-mount: Zeiss Touit 12mm f/2.8 at 22)

## Pros
- Compact and lightweight (225g) for an ultra-wide zoom
- Low distortion for the focal range (0.3% barrel at 10mm)
- Built-in OSS useful for handheld architecture/interiors
- Non-rotating 62mm filter ring — works well with polarisers/grads
- Excellent centre sharpness when stopped down to f/5.6–f/8

## Cons
- Soft edges wide open, especially at 14–18mm — needs f/8 for uniform sharpness
- Heavy vignetting at f/4 (up to 2.3EV in corners)
- AF can hunt in low contrast or low light
- Max aperture f/4 limits low-light handheld use
- Older design (2012) — outclassed optically by Sigma 10-18mm f/2.8 DC DN

## Best Use Cases
- Landscape photography (tripod, stopped down)
- Travel/street with Sony APS-C bodies (a6xxx series)
- Indoor architecture where tripods aren't allowed (OSS helps)
- Video on gimbals — light weight is an advantage
