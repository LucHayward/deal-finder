# Zeiss Loxia 21mm f/2.8 Lens for Sony E

Listing price: R10,000

DxOMark: 35 (tested on Sony A7R; best-in-class ultra-wide primes for Sony E score ~50+, e.g. Sony FE 12-24mm GM)

## Pros
- Outstanding sharpness wide-open (f/2.8 is genuinely usable across the frame)
- Exceptional distortion control — only 0.4%, no mustache distortion
- All-metal build, fully mechanical aperture and focus rings
- De-clickable aperture ring for video
- Compact and lightweight for a full-frame 21mm

## Cons
- Heavy vignetting (~2.8 EV in corners at f/2.8, persists stopped down)
- Manual focus only — no autofocus
- Some field curvature at landscape distances
- Aperture ring can shift accidentally (reported usability issue)
- Premium price for an f/2.8 max aperture

## Best Use Cases
- Landscape and architecture photography (excellent edge-to-edge sharpness)
- Astrophotography (low coma, good wide-open performance)
- Travel/street (compact, discreet build)
- Video (de-clickable aperture, smooth manual rings)
